## About

*Decisions Under Uncertainty* is a seven-chapter reading companion for **XQMB 5311 – Statistics for Business**, an accelerated course in the Executive MBA program at The University of Texas at El Paso.

The approach is decision-first: every statistical method is introduced through a real business problem where imprecise analysis has measurable consequences — in dollars, careers, and strategic direction. Rather than presenting formulas in the abstract, each chapter follows two EMBA students, David and Maria, as they work through consulting cases across industries ranging from manufacturing and pharmaceuticals to hospitality, digital marketing, and real estate.

The progression moves from description to probability to inference to comparison to regression — building an integrated analytical toolkit that executives can apply the moment they leave the classroom.

## Contents

| Lecture | Chapter | Case | Topics |
|:-------:|---------|------|--------|
| 1 | Descriptive Statistics | TechFlow Solutions | Measures of location, variability, distribution shape, correlation |
| 2 | Probability | PrecisionCast Industries | Conditional probability, Bayes' theorem, expected value, binomial and normal distributions |
| 3 | Interval Estimation and Hypothesis Testing | BorderMed Pharmaceuticals | Confidence intervals, hypothesis testing, p-values, Type I & II errors |
| 4 | ANOVA: Analysis of Variance | DesertVine Hospitality | One-way and two-way ANOVA, post-hoc comparisons, multiple testing |
| 5 | Categorical Data Analysis | PixelPerfect Marketing | Chi-square tests, confidence intervals for proportions, association vs. causation |
| 6 | Statistical Foundations Review | — | Method selection, interpretation errors, bridge to econometrics |
| 7 | Regression Analysis | Sunburst Homes Construction | OLS regression, coefficient interpretation, multicollinearity, heteroskedasticity |
